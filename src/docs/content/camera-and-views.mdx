# Camera & Views

## Camera Enabled

Turns on 3D-oriented viewing aids and orientation controls.

## Standard views

- Top
- Bottom
- Left
- Right
- Front
- Back

## View Cube

- Click a face to snap view
- Press-hold and drag to orient
- Release to settle/snap

## Coordinates

- **X = Red**
- **Y = Green**
- **Z = Blue**
